Help & Support

Find answers, guidance, and support to use KisanSathi effectively. We're here to help you make the most of our AI-powered agricultural platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers to help you get started

How do I register as a farmer?

Click on the "Register" button in the top navigation bar. Fill in your basic details including name, phone number, location, and farm information. You will receive a verification code via SMS. Enter the code to complete your registration. Once verified, you can access all features of KisanSathi.

How can I check government-verified crop prices?

After logging in, navigate to the "Crop Prices" section from your dashboard. Select your state and district to view current market prices. All prices are fetched directly from government databases and updated daily. You can also set price alerts for specific crops to get notifications when prices change.

How do I upload crop images for disease detection?

Go to the "Disease Detection" feature in your dashboard. Click on "Upload Image" and select a clear photo of the affected crop leaf or plant part. Ensure good lighting and focus. Our AI will analyze the image within seconds and provide disease identification along with treatment recommendations. You can also share these results with agricultural experts for verification.

How do I ask questions to agricultural experts?

Visit the "Expert Consultation" section. You can browse available experts by specialty (crop type, pest management, soil health, etc.). Click "Ask Expert" to submit your question with photos if needed. Experts typically respond within 24-48 hours. You can also join live Q&A sessions scheduled weekly on various agricultural topics.

How does the AI chatbot work?

Click on the chatbot icon at the bottom right of any page. Ask your farming questions in Hindi or English. The AI chatbot provides instant answers on topics like crop care, weather advisories, fertilizer use, pest control, and government schemes. For complex queries, the chatbot will connect you with a human expert. The service is available 24/7.

Is KisanSathi free to use?

Yes, KisanSathi is completely free for all registered farmers. This is a government-supported initiative to empower farmers with technology and expert knowledge. There are no hidden charges, subscription fees, or premium tiers. All features including AI analysis, expert consultations, and government scheme information are accessible to every farmer at no cost.

AI Feature Guidance

Understanding how our AI-powered tools can help you

Disease Detection

Upload clear images of affected plant parts. Our AI analyzes visual symptoms and identifies diseases with 85-90% accuracy. Results include disease name, severity level, and recommended treatments.

Best results with natural daylight photos

Fertilizer Recommendations

Input your soil test results, crop type, and growth stage. AI calculates optimal NPK ratios and suggests specific fertilizer products with application schedules tailored to your field conditions.

Get soil tested every 2-3 years

Yield Prediction

Based on crop variety, planting date, weather patterns, and farm practices, AI forecasts expected yield. This helps in planning harvest logistics, storage needs, and market timing for better prices.

Predictions improve with regular updates

AI Chatbot Assistance

Ask questions in simple language. The chatbot understands farming terminology in multiple languages and provides instant answers on crop care, pest control, weather advisories, and government schemes.

Available 24/7 in Hindi and English

Important Notice

AI-powered tools provide recommendations based on data analysis and past patterns. While highly accurate, they are meant to assist and guide your decisions, not replace your farming experience and judgment. Final decisions regarding your crops, inputs, and farm management remain with you. For critical issues, we recommend consulting with agricultural experts available through our platform.

Account & Security Help

Keeping your account safe and secure

Password Safety

  • • Use different passwords for different accounts

Account Verification
